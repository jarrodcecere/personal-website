About

Learning first. Technology with a purpose.

I’m an Instructional Designer in Seton Hall University’s Teaching, Learning and Technology Center. I’ve spent more than a decade in instructional design after beginning my career teaching psychology and other college courses. My work combines faculty consultation, course design, professional development, and instructional innovation across online, hybrid, and in-person learning environments.

I hold an M.S. in Experimental Psychology from Saint Joseph’s University and a Graduate Professional Development Certificate in Instructional Design from the University of Wisconsin–Stout. I’m also a Quality Matters Certified Peer Reviewer. My background in psychology and higher education teaching continues to shape how I approach learning design: start with the learner and the instructional goal, then choose the methods and technologies that best support them.
